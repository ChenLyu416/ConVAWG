# ConVAWG Data Use Agreement

*Version 1.1.*

The ConVAWG dataset ("the Dataset") consists of synthetic chat dialogues and
associated metadata depicting Violence Against Women and Girls (VAWG)
scenarios. It contains abusive, coercive and potentially distressing language.
All content is machine-generated; it does not contain messages written by, or
records about, real individuals.

By submitting the access form, or by downloading or using the Dataset, you
("the Recipient") agree to the following terms. If you accept on behalf of an
institution, you confirm that you are authorised to do so.

## 1. Permitted use

The Dataset may be used only for **non-commercial research and education**
aimed at understanding, detecting, analysing or preventing abuse, including
the development and evaluation of detection, moderation, safeguarding and
safety-evaluation methods.

## 2. Prohibited use

The Recipient must not use the Dataset, or anything derived from it, to:

1. harass, threaten, intimidate or abuse any person, or to produce content for
   that purpose;
2. profile, identify, assess or make inferences about real individuals;
3. train, fine-tune or evaluate systems whose primary purpose is to generate
   abusive, coercive or harassing content;
4. inform or make decisions in real-world cases — including policing, legal,
   safeguarding, clinical or risk-assessment decisions about actual people;
5. pursue any commercial purpose without prior written permission from the
   authors.

## 3. Redistribution

The Recipient must not redistribute the Dataset, in whole or in substantial
part, to anyone who has not accepted this agreement; direct others to the dataset
page instead. Short
excerpts may be reproduced in research publications, with a content warning,
for illustration. Models trained on the Dataset may be shared only where doing
so is consistent with Section 2.

## 4. Handling

The Recipient will store the Dataset with access limited to people who have
themselves accepted this agreement, will label any derived data as synthetic, and will make
collaborators who are exposed to the content aware of its nature in advance.

## 5. Attribution

Publications and other outputs that use the Dataset must cite the ConVAWG
paper (see the repository README).

## 6. Revocation

Access may be revoked if these terms are breached. On revocation the Recipient
must delete all copies of the Dataset and stop using it.

## 7. No warranty

The Dataset is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It is synthetic
and is not a statistically representative sample of real abuse; conclusions
about real-world prevalence or individual cases must not be drawn from it. The
authors accept no liability for any use of the Dataset.

## 8. Changes to these terms

The authors may publish a revised version of this agreement. The version
accepted by the Recipient continues to apply to copies obtained under it.

## Access

Access is through the form on the dataset page
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/ChenL49/ConVAWG). The form records the
Recipient's name, affiliation, country, position and intended use, together
with acceptance of these terms. Submitting it constitutes acceptance, and
access is granted immediately. The information is visible to the dataset
authors and is used only to administer access.
